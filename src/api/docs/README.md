## What is the Lombok Halal Room API?

This API is exclusively designed to empower our website and Android app, "Lombok Halal Room." We are thrilled to provide you with the tools and resources needed to seamlessly integrate and enhance your experience with our platform.

## Key Features

- **Seamless Integration**: Integrate our API into your applications, websites, or services with ease, opening up new possibilities for your projects.

- **Authentication Made Simple**: Our API offers straightforward authentication methods, allowing you to get started quickly and securely.

- **Extensive Endpoint Coverage**: Explore a variety of endpoints that cater to different use cases, whether you need details on specific accommodations or a comprehensive overview of Halal options in the region.
